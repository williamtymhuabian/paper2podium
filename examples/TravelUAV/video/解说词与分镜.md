| 04 | 0:51.8–1:12.3 | 02 DATA | 20.5s | Human pilots fly each trajectory, while sensor streams are recorded asynchronously. GPT-4 writes target descriptions, checked by humans. | 05 | 1:12.3–1:30.3 | 03 BENCHMARK | 18.0s | UAV-Need-Help turns assistance into a benchmark. Level one gives full guidance. Level two helps only at critical states. Level three warns about collisions. The policy must search from the description, the scene, and whatever help it receives. |
| 06 | 1:30.3–1:52.2 | 04 METHOD | 21.9s | The method is hierarchical. An MLLM fuses the instruction with five RGB views.
